From the outside, it's just a shed. Inside, it's a workshop where sawdust and circuit boards share the same bench.

**A Shed Called Skynet** is where I make things: hand-finished woodwork, precise CNC-cut pieces, and robotics projects that move, sense and occasionally misbehave. Some builds start with a sketch and a chisel. Others start with a toolpath, a microcontroller and a lot of trial and error. Most end up somewhere in between.

Here you'll find finished projects, works in progress, build notes and the odd experiment that didn't quite go to plan. Whether you're into joinery, machining, electronics or just like seeing how things are made, pull up a stool.

Don't worry, it's only planning to take over the garden.

## What's in the shed

- 🪵 **Woodwork**: hand tools, joinery and finishing
- ⚙️ **CNC**: toolpaths, cut files and machined parts
- 🤖 **Robotics**: electronics, code and things that move
- 🧪 **Experiments**: everything else
