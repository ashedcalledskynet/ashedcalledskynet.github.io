## About this policy

This policy explains how personal information is handled when you visit **ashedcalledskynet.co.uk**. This policy applies only to ashedcalledskynet.co.uk and does not extend to other domains or independently operated websites, even if they use the same or a similar name. The website is operated by its owner, who is responsible for the personal information they process in connection with it.

## Information collected

The website is designed as a static website. It does not provide visitor accounts, comments, advertising or visitor analytics.

The website is intended to be hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses and request information, to deliver the website, maintain security and operate its services. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for details.

Domain registration and DNS service providers may also process technical information as necessary to operate the domain and resolve website addresses.

## Email correspondence

If you contact the website owner by email, your email address, message and any information you choose to provide may be processed to respond to your enquiry and manage correspondence. The lawful basis is legitimate interests in communicating with visitors and operating the website, where those interests are not overridden by your rights.

Correspondence is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for those purposes or to meet applicable legal obligations. Email service providers may process information to deliver and store correspondence.

## Cookies and similar technologies

The website does not intentionally set first-party tracking cookies or use advertising or analytics trackers. Hosting and other service providers may use technologies necessary to operate and protect their services.

Fonts used by the website are hosted locally, so displaying the website's text does not require a connection to an external font service.

## YouTube videos

Some pages may contain YouTube videos. Videos are not loaded automatically. A notice and a **Load video** button are displayed instead.

If you choose to load a video, your browser connects to YouTube (Google). Google may receive technical information about your visit and use cookies or similar technologies. This processing is subject to Google's own policies.

You can avoid this connection by leaving the video unloaded. For further information, see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's cookie information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies).

## External links

The website may link to other websites and services. Following an external link takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices. This policy does not cover those services.

## International transfers

Hosting, domain, email and optional third-party video services may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where the website owner arranges a restricted transfer of personal information, applicable UK data protection requirements must be met, including appropriate safeguards where required. Third-party providers describe their own processing and transfer arrangements in their privacy policies.

Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. These rights are subject to applicable conditions and exceptions.

You may also complain to the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk/).
